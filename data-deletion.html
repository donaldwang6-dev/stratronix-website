Data Deletion Instructions

Version 1.0  ·  Effective 22 November 2026  ·  Last reviewed 22 November 2026

This page explains how to withdraw authorisation and request deletion of data that STRATRONIX Technology (Shenzhen) Company, Limited has received through a connected social media platform account. It applies to LinkedIn, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), YouTube, X and any other platform we connect using that platform's official API.

1. Immediate Self-Service Revocation

The fastest way to stop our access is to revoke the application in the platform's own settings. Revocation takes effect immediately and invalidates the access token our platform holds.

PlatformWhere to revoke
LinkedInSettings & Privacy → Data privacy → Permitted services → find the STRATRONIX application → Remove
Meta (Facebook / Instagram)Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → find STRATRONIX → Remove
Google / YouTubemyaccount.google.com/permissions → find STRATRONIX → Remove access
X (Twitter)Settings and privacy → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → Revoke access
Other platformsAccount settings → Connected apps / Authorised applications → remove STRATRONIX

Where a platform provides a data-deletion callback or a signed request for deletion, our platform supports it and will process the request on receipt.

2. Requesting Deletion From Us

To request erasure of data we hold, email support@stratronix.ai with the subject line "Data Request" and include:

  1. the name of the platform and the account (Page, channel or profile) concerned;
  2. the account identifier shown in the platform, if available;
  3. confirmation of the action requested — for example "delete all data obtained through this connection";
  4. a contact address for our reply.

Alternative channel: if you cannot use email, write to STRATRONIX Technology (Shenzhen) Company, Limited, Room 1203D, Building C6, Hengfeng Industrial Park, No. 739 Zhoushi Road, Hangcheng Subdistrict, Bao'an District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China, marking the envelope "Privacy Request". Postal requests are logged on receipt.

3. What We Delete

Data categoryAction
OAuth access and refresh tokensErased immediately, irreversibly.
Account identifiers, names, permission scopesErased, unless retention is legally required.
Publishing records linked to the connectionErased or irreversibly anonymised within 30 days.
Operational logs containing an identifierIdentifier removed; the anonymised technical record expires under the normal 12-month cycle.

Content that was already published on a third-party platform is controlled by that platform, not by us. Deleting our data does not remove published posts from the platform — you must delete those in the platform itself (for a LinkedIn Page: Admin view → the post → delete). Where a platform API allows us to delete a post we published, we will do so on request.

4. How Long It Takes

  • Acknowledgement — within 5 business days of receipt.
  • Action — within 30 days where verification is straightforward, or within any shorter period required by applicable law (for example, GDPR requires one month, extendable by two months for complex requests with notice).
  • Verification — we may need to confirm you are authorised to act for the account concerned. We will tell you what is needed and will not use the request itself for any other purpose.

5. Limited Retention

A small amount of data may be retained where required by law (for example tax and accounting records), where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or where necessary to keep a security incident record. Retained data is limited to that purpose and protected by the same safeguards described in our Privacy Policy.

6. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with our handling of a request, contact us first at support@stratronix.ai so that we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority at any time.
