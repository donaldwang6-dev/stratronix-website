Why PAA Wins on Cost

Three structural advantages drive the savings:

  • Hardware is one-time: STA-100 hardware cost is a CapEx purchase. After Year 1, you have a fleet of edge appliances with no per-user fee.
  • Cloud LLM is elastic: Pay only for inference calls. Off-hours (nights, weekends) can drive usage to near-zero for many enterprise workflows.
  • OpenClaw is free: The AI-agent runtime is Apache 2.0 open-source. Zero license fees forever.

Hidden Costs of GPU Servers

GPU servers look like the "cheapest" option at first glance, but the hidden costs add up. Each H100 80GB costs $25K-$40K. A production cluster needs 4-8 GPUs, plus NVMe storage, redundant power, cooling infrastructure. Add 1-2 FTE sysadmins at $100K-$200K each. Plus LLM hosting (the model still needs to come from somewhere). Most enterprises underestimate GPU server TCO by 3-5×.

Hidden Costs of SaaS (Per-Seat AI)

Per-seat SaaS AI ($30-60/seat/month) appears cheap at 5 users. At 250+ users, the bill explodes. Worse: SaaS AI providers charge per-seat even when employees are travelling, on leave, or rarely using AI. Several enterprises have reported 6-figure overruns when scaling AI pilots.
