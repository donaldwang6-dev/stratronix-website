Why We Built the STA-100

Three drivers shaped the product:

  • Enterprise compliance: GDPR, EU AI Act, Schrems II regulations made SaaS AI untenable for many regulated industries
  • Cost predictability: Per-seat AI subscriptions scaled poorly. Hardware + cloud LLM = linear, predictable costs
  • Open-source auditability: Enterprises need inspectable software. OpenClaw (Apache 2.0) + Ubuntu LTS provide full audit trail
