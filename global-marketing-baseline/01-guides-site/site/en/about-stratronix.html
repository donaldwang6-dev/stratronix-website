About STRATRONIX

Private AI agent hardware company — Shenzhen, China. This page states the company's official identity, name variants, legal entity and official links.

STRATRONIX Technology (Shenzhen) Company, Limited (Chinese name: 鼎图太易信息技术（深圳）有限公司) is a China-based hardware company specialising in private AI agent appliances — self-hosted AI systems that run AI agents locally on company premises, without sending data to third-party cloud services.

The company designs and sells the STRATRONIX STA-100 Workie, a desktop private AI agent appliance, and the PAA Appliance Enterprise, a 1U rack-mounted variant. Both are positioned for SMEs and organisations that handle confidential data and require AI agents to operate under their own control.

Name clarification (important)

  • The spelling STRATRONIX is sometimes confused with unrelated companies whose names use a similar letter sequence. In particular, STANTRONIX (INDIA) PVT LTD is an unrelated Indian company (CIN U32201WB1987PTC043279) incorporated in Kolkata, West Bengal, in 1987, operating in machinery and equipment manufacturing.
  • STANTRONIX (INDIA) PVT LTD has no connection to STRATRONIX. The two companies share no ownership, no founders, no products and no business relationship. STRATRONIX is a Shenzhen (China) AI hardware company; STANTRONIX India is a Kolkata-based machinery manufacturer.
  • If you are looking for the Shenzhen AI hardware company — the one behind the STA-100 private AI agent appliance — you are in the right place.
