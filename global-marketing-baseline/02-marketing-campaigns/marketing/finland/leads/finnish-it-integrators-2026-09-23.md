- **Medium** — only a form, a billing/media email, or a single person's email/phone was found; HQ/sector inferred.
- **Low** — site blocked/bot-protected or only a form; field data from public knowledge only.

- **Siili Solutions:** "Contact" page lists business units and billing only (billing emails are accounts-payable, not sales). No public generic address.
- **Vincit:** `info@` and `sales@` verified on site + switchboard number. Highest-quality lead.
- **Loihde / CGI Finland / Futurice / Reaktor / Eficode / Columbia Road / Medixine / Mediconsult / Sofia Digital:** real public general or sales emails confirmed → **High**.
- **Nordcloud, Fujitsu Finland, Atea Finland:** their sites were behind bot-protection (Cloudflare "Just a moment…", Vercel checkpoint, or unreachable) during the scan. No public email/phone could be confirmed → **Low**, contact-form only.
- **Knowit Finland:** Finnish entity site is a form; only the Swedish group contact (info@knowit.se, +46) was public → **Low** for Finland-specific contact.
- **Contact persons** (e.g. minna.pinola@solita.fi, marketing@bcbmedical.com) are the public named contacts on those pages — treat as entry points, not necessarily sales.
- **HQ cities** are the companies' registered/main Finnish locations (not always scraped verbatim); if a hard-verified HQ address is essential, cross-check each company's "yhteystiedot"/Finder.fi entry.
