## 📝 LinkedIn Post Draft (English)

> # Why On-Device AI Will Eat $50B Cloud AI Market by 2030
> 
> Two years ago, every AI demo started with "let me open ChatGPT."
> 
> Today, the smartest CTOs I talk to are saying the opposite:
> 
> *"Get our data out of the cloud. We need local."*
> 
> Here's what's happening 👇
> 
> ---
> 
> ## The Cloud AI Bill Is Getting Out of Hand
> 
> A mid-size law firm (45 lawyers) told me last quarter:
> 
> → $14,000/month on OpenAI + Anthropic APIs
> → 80% of prompts: contract review, document drafting, email triage
> → 0% of that data needs to leave their network
> 
> They were paying AWS prices to run what is essentially a search box.
> 
> And they're not alone. The "AI bill shock" is real:
> 
> - Average enterprise AI spend grew 4.7× in 2025
> - 60% of CIOs cite AI compute as their #1 cost concern
> - CFOs are asking the question nobody wants to answer: "What did we actually get?"
> 
> ---
> 
> ## The Privacy Hammer Is Coming Down
> 
> Meanwhile, three things happened:
> 
> 1. **EU AI Act** (Aug 2024) — high-risk AI processing requires local data control
> 2. **GDPR enforcement** — 2025 fines crossed €5.9B, 32% YoY increase
> 3. **State-level US laws** — CCPA, Colorado AI Act, NY SHIELD all tightening
> 
> Result? Lawyers, doctors, accountants, and government contractors are being TOLD:
> 
> *"You cannot send client data to OpenAI."*
> 
> Not "shouldn't." **Cannot.**
> 
> ---
> 
> ## The Hardware Moment
> 
> While the cloud was busy training the next 1T-parameter model, three things converged on-device:
> 
> | Capability | 2020 | 2026 |
> |-----------|------|------|
> | Llama 3 70B inference speed | 0.3 tok/s | 12 tok/s |
> | 8B model on consumer GPU | impossible | 2 sec TTFT |
> | Power efficiency | 350W | 75W |
> | Memory for 70B | $30,000 H100 | $399 box |
> 
> That last row is the one nobody is talking about loud enough.
> 
> A $399 device today runs a 70B-parameter LLM at usable speeds, with zero data leaving your building.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## The 2030 Math
> 
> Cloud AI market today: ~$50B (inference, not training).
> 
> Of that, **60-70% is "private knowledge work"** — the kind of prompts that never needed to leave the building:
> 
> - Email drafts
> - Contract reviews  
> - Customer support transcripts
> - Internal Q&A
> - Document summarization
> 
> If 80% of that workload moves on-device by 2030 (which is what current adoption curves suggest):
> 
> → **$24-28B will shift from cloud to local**
> 
> That's not "AI eats software."
> 
> That's **AI eats the cloud bill.**
> 
> ---
> 
> ## What This Means for You
> 
> If you're a CTO making AI bets for 2026-2030:
> 
> ✅ **Audit your prompts.** Anything that doesn't need to leave your network, shouldn't.
> 
> ✅ **Plan for hybrid.** Best-in-class for public knowledge (cloud) + best-in-class for private knowledge (local).
> 
> ✅ **Buy hardware, not just API credits.** CapEx is back. Your finance team will thank you when the API bill stops growing.
> 
> ✅ **Start small.** A $399 box today can replace $3,000/month in API spend for most SME workloads.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## The Bigger Picture
> 
> Cloud AI won the 2020s because models were too big to run anywhere else.
> 
> On-device AI wins the 2030s because **most of what you actually do with AI doesn't need a trillion-parameter model**.
> 
> The future is hybrid:
> - Cloud for the long tail of public knowledge
> - Local for everything private, fast, and routine
> 
> And the companies that figure this out first will spend 90% less than their competitors.
>
