Yesterday a friend asked me about AI for his new website.

I demoed STA-100 for 30 minutes. I showed him the device, the workflows, the price ($369).

He still chose Tencent Cloud.

Not because Tencent was better. Because **it was easier to say "yes"**.

---

That conversation taught me something important:
**For non-technical users, "decision cost" beats "feature depth".**

Cloud AI wins on:
✅ 5-minute signup
✅ Free trial
✅ Pay-as-you-go

Local AI wins on:
✅ Data sovereignty
✅ Long-term TCO
✅ No vendor lock-in

But it loses on:
❌ Perceived "hassle" of buying hardware
❌ Higher upfront cost

---

If you're an SMB / law firm / hospital / SMB IT lead — and you care about data sovereignty, audit trail, and TCO — local-first AI is for you.

If you're a solo founder who just needs a chatbot — cloud AI is fine.

**Choose based on what you're optimizing for.**
