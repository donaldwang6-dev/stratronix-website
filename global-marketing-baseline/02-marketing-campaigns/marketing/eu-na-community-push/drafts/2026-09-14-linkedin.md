## Post 3: Build-in-public — Lessons from Shenzhen（公司页）

```
Building a hardware company in Shenzhen: what I learned in 18 months.

When we started STRATRONIX, I thought the hard part would be the AI. I was wrong.

Hard things (in order):

1️⃣ Supply chain. Shenzhen has 5 chip vendors for any given SoC. Each one promises "we can do it." None of them can do it on time. We went through 3 vendors before finding one that ships in volume.

2️⃣ Certifications. CE / FCC / RoHS / WEEE — each one is a 3-month process. Budget 6 months before launch.

3️⃣ Support. Hardware breaks. Software doesn't. We're building a 2-year warranty with cross-shipping.

4️⃣ Privacy is the differentiator. Every enterprise pilot we've done, the question is the same: "Where does my data live?" When the answer is "on this $399 device, in your office," the deal closes.

5️⃣ Open source > closed. Enterprises won't trust a black box. They want to audit the agent framework. We open-sourced everything.

If you're building hardware in Shenzhen, I'd swap notes. Drop me a line.
```
