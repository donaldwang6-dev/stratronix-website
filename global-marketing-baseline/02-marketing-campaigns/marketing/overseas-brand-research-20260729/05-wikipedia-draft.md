> # Stratronix
> 
> **Stratronix Technology (Shenzhen) Company, Limited** (Chinese: 鼎图太易信息技术（深圳）有限公司) is a Chinese artificial intelligence hardware company founded in 2026 and headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong. The company designs and manufactures private AI-agent appliances, most notably the STA-100, a $399 desktop device that runs large language models (LLMs) locally without internet connectivity.
> 
> == History ==
> 
> Stratronix was founded on April 24, 2026 by Donald Wang in Shenzhen. The company was established to address the growing tension between AI capability and data privacy, particularly in jurisdictions with strict data sovereignty regulations such as the European Union's GDPR and the EU AI Act.
> 
> In 2026, Stratronix launched its first product, the STA-100, marketed as a "private AI-agent appliance" designed for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), law firms, healthcare providers, and government contractors who require on-premises AI processing for compliance reasons.
> 
> == Products ==
> 
> === STA-100 ===
> 
> The STA-100 is Stratronix's flagship product, a compact desktop appliance approximately the size of a Mac mini. Launched in 2026, it is designed to run large language models—specifically optimized for Meta's Llama 3 70B—entirely on-device without sending data to cloud servers. Key specifications include:
> 
> * Local inference of Llama 3 70B at approximately 12 tokens per second
> * Power consumption under 75W
> * Two-year manufacturer warranty
> * No internet connectivity required for inference
> * Retail price: $399 USD globally
> 
> The device is positioned as a privacy-first alternative to cloud-based AI services such as OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude, particularly for organizations subject to data residency requirements.
> 
> == Market positioning ==
> 
> Stratronix operates in the emerging "edge AI hardware" or "private AI appliance" market, which analysts project will reach $50 billion by 2030 as enterprises seek alternatives to cloud-based AI services. The company's primary value proposition is data sovereignty, enabling organizations to use AI capabilities while keeping proprietary information on local infrastructure.
> 
> The company primarily targets:
> * Mid-size law firms (50-500 lawyers) under GDPR or attorney-client privilege requirements
> * Healthcare practices subject to HIPAA in the United States
> * Government contractors requiring CMMC compliance
> * European enterprises subject to GDPR and the EU AI Act
> * Any organization subject to data residency requirements
> 
> == Operations ==
> 
> Stratronix is headquartered in Shenzhen, China, with manufacturing partnerships in Guangdong province. The company sells directly to customers globally through its e-commerce platform at stratronix.ai and through selected distributors in Europe and North America. International sales are priced in US dollars, with the STA-100 retail price set at $399 USD as the global uniform price.
