1. The European Compliance Landscape (2026)

Three regulatory layers affect any AI deployment in the EU:

  • GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) — personal data residency, lawful basis, data subject rights
  • EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) — risk-classified AI system requirements; full enforcement August 2026
  • Schrems II / EDPB Recommendations — transfer impact assessments for any non-EU processor

2. PAA Architecture: Local Data, Cloud Inference (in EU)

STRATRONIX STA-100 is a compact edge appliance (8-core ARM + 4GB RAM) running OpenClaw, the open-source AI-agent runtime (Apache 2.0), on Ubuntu 24.04 LTS. The device lives on your office network and stores everything locally:

3. GDPR Compliance Mapping

Article 28 (Processor)

Under a standard PAA deployment, your organisation is the data controller. The cloud LLM provider acts as processor under their published DPA. Stratronix is neither controller nor processor for customer data — we ship hardware and open-source software only. This eliminates the Schrems II transfer-impact-assessment burden that arises when a non-EU SaaS provider is the processor.

4. EU AI Act 2026 Compliance

STRATRONIX STA-100 PAA architecture supports compliance for both Limited Risk (transparency obligations) and High Risk (Annex III use cases — credit scoring, employment, law enforcement, etc.). OpenClaw logs every agent decision; exportable for audit.
