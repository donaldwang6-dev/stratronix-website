1. Legal — Law Firms (5-200 lawyers)

Workflow patterns

  • Document review and redaction — load case files (PDF, DOCX) into on-device RAG; ask questions; get answers with citations
  • Contract drafting assistance — start with firm precedents (stored locally); agent suggests clauses; lawyer approves
  • Case law research — query against local case database; results cite original paragraphs
  • Client communication drafting — letters, emails, status updates; human review before sending

Why PAA, not ChatGPT Enterprise?

  • Privileged client documents never leave the office
  • Bar association / Law Society ethical obligations met (no third-party processor)
  • Per-client data segregation (each case in its own encrypted folder)
  • Audit trail of every agent decision for malpractice defence

Compliance posture

GDPR Article 28 (processor obligations) + national bar council rules + client confidentiality under attorney-client privilege. PAA architecture ensures the law firm remains the sole data controller for client matters.

2. Healthcare — Clinics, Hospitals, Research Labs

Workflow patterns

  • Clinical note summarisation — dictated encounter notes → structured SOAP notes
  • Patient education material generation — condition-specific handouts in patient's preferred language
  • Medical literature search — query against local journal subscriptions
  • Multidisciplinary team meeting prep — summarise patient histories for tumour boards, etc.

Why PAA, not Azure OpenAI?

  • Patient PHI never leaves the clinic's network
  • HIPAA-compliant BAA only covers US providers; PAA eliminates the need
  • GDPR Article 9 (special category data) processing on-premises
  • Cloud LLM can be EU-jurisdiction (Mistral, Aleph Alpha) for additional safety

Compliance posture

GDPR Article 9 + national health data laws + medical professional confidentiality + (where applicable) HIPAA via jurisdiction-adequate cloud LLM. PAA keeps patient data on-device while AI inference happens in EU-jurisdiction clouds with strong data processing agreements.

3. Finance — Banks, Insurance, Asset Management

Workflow patterns

  • KYC / AML document review — flag suspicious patterns in customer documentation
  • Regulatory reporting assistance — MiFID II, Basel III, Solvency II templates
  • Investment research synthesis — query Bloomberg-style research notes stored locally
  • Customer service agent assist — call centre agents get real-time suggestions

Why PAA, not internal GPU server?

  • $399 vs $30,000 — viable for branch offices
  • No DevOps hiring — managed via OpenClaw OTA updates
  • Cloud LLM can be EU-jurisdiction for ECB outsourcing guidelines
  • Per-branch data isolation for ring-fencing obligations

Compliance posture

GDPR + PSD2 + MiFID II + national financial regulator requirements (BaFin, AMF, Banca d'Italia, etc.). PAA architecture supports the ECB's outsourcing guidelines (cloud LLM with EU DPA, financial institution remains data controller).

4. Government — Public Administration

Workflow patterns

  • Citizen service desk assistance — first-line agents handle 70% of common questions
  • Document translation (official EU languages) — on-device terminology base; cloud LLM for fluency
  • Procurement / RFP response drafting — query against past tenders
  • Internal policy Q&A — public servants query regulations via natural language

Why PAA, not Azure OpenAI Service?

  • Government documents stay inside the agency's network
  • No US CLOUD Act exposure
  • Audit trail for freedom-of-information requests
  • Citizen data protection under ePrivacy + national laws

Compliance posture

GDPR + ePrivacy Directive + national public administration data laws + EU AI Act high-risk requirements (Annex III — administration of justice, public services). PAA supports compliance by keeping sensitive data on-premises while AI inference uses EU-jurisdiction clouds.

5. Manufacturing — Operations, Supply Chain

Workflow patterns

  • Production line troubleshooting — operator describes fault; agent retrieves from maintenance KB
  • Quality report generation — IoT sensor data → structured daily quality summaries
  • Supplier onboarding documentation — query against compliance checklists
  • Multi-language shift handover — German / Polish / Romanian / Italian factory floor crews

Why PAA, not cloud AI suite?

  • Proprietary process IP stays on factory network
  • Air-gap friendly (local automation works without internet for non-AI workflows)
  • EUR/USD pricing — same $399 globally, simplifies multi-country rollouts
  • Low power consumption (15W) — no factory floor UPS upgrade needed

Compliance posture

Industry-specific (CE marking, ISO 9001, IATF 16949, FDA for pharma) + GDPR for personal data in HR/quality records. PAA keeps proprietary process documentation on-device.

6. Cross-Industry Lessons (2026 EU Deployments)

From our customer deployments in Q1-Q2 2026, three patterns repeat across industries:

  1. Pilot in one team, expand to organisation. Most successful deployments start with a single team (e.g., legal documentation, customer service, or factory maintenance), prove the value, then expand.
  2. Cloud LLM choice is a strategic decision. Customers typically choose their cloud LLM based on existing enterprise contracts (Azure, AWS, Google Cloud) or sovereignty preferences (Mistral, Aleph Alpha).
  3. Compliance framing accelerates procurement. Positioning PAA as "no third-party data processor, you are the data controller" shortens legal review from 6 months to 6 weeks.
