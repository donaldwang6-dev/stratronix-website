2. What the STA-100 Does

The STA-100 is an edge appliance that lives in your office. It runs the open-source OpenClaw agent runtime and connects your team to any major cloud LLM (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Aleph Alpha, Qwen, DeepSeek, Llama via Bedrock, etc.) using your enterprise API key.

Three things it does:

  1. Stores your data on-device. Chat history, RAG documents, agent workflows, integrations — all encrypted on the device's SSD. No third party holds your data.
  2. Routes AI requests to your cloud LLM. You choose the provider (EU/US jurisdiction as you prefer); the PAA is the secure edge gateway.
  3. Integrates with your team's tools. Feishu, WeChat Work, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, WhatsApp — bind in 10 seconds via QR scan.

3. Who It's For

  • European SMBs (10-500 employees) needing GDPR-compliant AI without $50K GPU server investment
  • Law firms, medical practices, accounting firms handling confidential client data
  • Regulated industries (financial services, healthcare, legal, government) requiring audit trails
  • Multi-branch enterprises wanting per-site data sovereignty
  • Privacy-conscious teams in any industry that prefer not to share prompts with SaaS providers

4. Who It's Not For

For engineering integrity, the STA-100 is not designed for classified / secret data environments that require fully air-gapped AI inference. The device requires internet connectivity to call cloud LLM endpoints for AI reasoning. Local compute handles routing, redaction, and small-class embedding tasks only. If your environment requires fully local LLM inference with no network, please contact our solutions team for a custom architecture.

8. Open-Source Commitment

STRATRONIX commits to keeping OpenClaw (the agent runtime inside STA-100) fully open-source under Apache 2.0 license. Every line of code that runs on the device is auditable. Security researchers can inspect, modify, and self-host the runtime. This is a deliberate architectural choice: closed-source AI infrastructure creates vendor lock-in and unverifiable security claims. Open-source is the only path to enterprise trust at scale.
