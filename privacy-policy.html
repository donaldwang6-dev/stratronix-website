Scope in one sentence: this policy covers the data we handle when our own corporate social accounts are connected to our platform. We do not operate a public social network, we do not sell software to consumers, and we do not collect data about members of the public through third-party platforms.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you authorise us to receive

When an administrator of our organisation connects a platform account (for example, a LinkedIn Page, YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram professional account or X account), that platform asks you to approve a specific set of permissions. Subject to your approval, we may receive:

  • Account identifiers — the numeric or opaque ID of the organisation account and the authorised administrator, together with the account display name and profile image URL.
  • OAuth access tokens — the credentials issued by the platform that allow our platform to act on the authorised account. We also receive refresh tokens where the platform issues them.
  • Permission scopes — the list of permissions you granted, which we store so that we can show you exactly what access is active and detect when it changes.
  • Organisation details — for example the Page name, vanity URL, industry category and logo, to the extent the authorised scope returns them.

2.2 Information we generate ourselves

  • Content and assets — marketing copy, images, videos and captions that our team creates or generates in the platform, including the review and approval decisions recorded against them.
  • Publishing records — timestamps, the target channel, the platform-assigned post identifier returned after publication, and the delivery status or error message.
  • Operational logs — technical records of API calls, such as request time, endpoint, response status and error codes, kept for troubleshooting, security and audit purposes.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect or process, through connected platform APIs: private messages of individuals, contact lists, connection graphs, followers' personal data, payment card data, government identity documents, or precise geolocation. We do not use cookies or tracking pixels on any third-party platform, and we do not build advertising profiles of platform members.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 applies, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (operating the platform for our organisation), legitimate interests (securing our systems, keeping audit records, and communicating about our brand — balanced against your rights), consent (where you approve an OAuth authorisation, which you may withdraw at any time), and legal obligation (record-keeping required by law).

5. How We Protect the Data

We treat OAuth tokens as secrets and apply the following measures:

  • Encryption in transit — all communication between our platform and platform APIs uses TLS.
  • Restricted, encrypted storage — tokens are stored on servers we control, in files readable only by the service account that needs them, with storage-level encryption.
  • Least privilege — we request only the permission scopes required for publishing and status retrieval, and we do not request scopes that would grant access to private messages or member personal data.
  • No token exposure — tokens are never included in logs, never displayed in full in our interface, and never transmitted to any party other than the platform that issued them.
  • Access control and audit — platform access is limited to named personnel who require it, protected by individual authentication, with actions recorded in an audit trail.
  • Retention limits — tokens are deleted when the connection is removed or the underlying authorisation is revoked, and in any event when no longer needed (see section 8).

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We monitor our systems and will notify affected parties and relevant authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires it.

7. International Transfers

Our systems are operated from the People's Republic of China and may use service providers in other jurisdictions. Where we transfer personal data outside the jurisdiction in which it was collected, we implement appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses or an equivalent lawful transfer mechanism, and we limit transfers to what is necessary for the purposes in section 3.

8. Retention

  • OAuth tokens and account identifiers — retained only while the connection is active. Deleted promptly after you remove the connection or the platform reports that the authorisation was revoked or expired beyond renewal.
  • Published content and publishing records — retained while the content remains part of our marketing record, normally not longer than 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Operational and audit logs — retained for up to 12 months for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

Where a legal hold, an audit requirement or a legal obligation applies, we may retain specific records for longer, limited to the required scope and period.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; rectify inaccurate data; erase data; restrict or object to processing; port data you provided; withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing); and lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

You can exercise these rights, and specifically withdraw your authorisation or request deletion of data obtained through a connected platform, in either of these ways:

  1. At the source — revoke our application in the platform's own settings (for LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services; for Meta: Settings → Business integrations; for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revocation immediately invalidates the token. You may also use the platform's own data-deletion callback where offered.
  2. By contacting us — email support@stratronix.ai with the subject line "Data Request". See our Data Deletion page for the exact procedure and response times.

We respond to verified requests within 30 days, or within the shorter period required by applicable law. We may ask for information sufficient to verify that you are authorised to act for the account concerned. Exercising these rights is free of charge.

10. Security of Third-Party Accounts

Connecting an account grants our platform the permissions you approve. You should grant access only to administrators authorised to act for your organisation, review the active permissions periodically, and revoke access immediately if you suspect compromise. STRATRONIX cannot control, and is not responsible for, the security practices of third-party platforms.

11. Children

Our platform is a business tool for organisations. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child's data has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Automated Processing and AI

We do not use automated processing to make decisions about individuals that produce legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not use connected-platform data to train models offered to third parties.
